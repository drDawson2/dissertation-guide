How AI Fits: The Colleague Model

You explain
your work
→
AI asks
questions
→
You make
decisions

AI serves as:

  • Strategic consultant
    (not a ghostwriter)
  • Thinking partner
    (not a decision maker)
  • Structure builder
    (not content generator)
  • Accountability coach
    (not task automator)

Academic Integrity: What AI Does

✓ Appropriate Uses
  • Provides organizational frameworks
  • Offers synthesis strategies
  • Asks clarifying questions to refine YOUR thinking
  • Identifies gaps in YOUR reasoning

Academic Integrity: What AI Does NOT Do

✗ Inappropriate Uses
  • Write your dissertation
  • Analyze your research data
  • Make methodological decisions for you
  • Replace your scholarly judgment
The Test
If you can't explain a concept without referencing the AI conversation,
you've crossed from consultation into dependence
