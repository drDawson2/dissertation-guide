Academic Integrity: What AI Does

✓ Appropriate Uses
  • Provides organizational frameworks
  • Offers synthesis strategies
  • Asks clarifying questions to refine YOUR thinking
  • Identifies gaps in YOUR reasoning

Academic Integrity: What AI Does NOT Do

✗ Inappropriate Uses
  • Write your dissertation
  • Analyze your research data
  • Make methodological decisions for you
  • Replace your scholarly judgment
The Test
If you can't explain a concept without referencing AI,
you've crossed from consultation into dependence

Step 7: Connect the Systems

Install Zotero Integration Plugin

  • Obsidian: Settings → Community Plugins

  • Test: Open Zotero + Command Palette (Ctrl/Cmd+P)

Step 8: Create Literature Note

The Process

  • Pick one source from Zotero
  • Create note in Obsidian: 01 - Literature Notes
  • Use Zotero Integration to import citation
  • Apply your annotation template
  • Work through source, filling each section
  • Link to concept notes as connections emerge
Not summarizing → Processing
Active scholarship, developing expertise
